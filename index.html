about

A tester with an engineer's foundation.

I have spent more than 15 years making complex software safe to ship, from payment systems and mobile apps to contact center platforms.

I started as a software engineer, and that shapes how I test. I build automation frameworks, read the architecture, and look for risk in the places where services, events and integrations meet.

Today I lead QA and release management at Competella in Stockholm. I set the test strategy, keep the release train on time, and mentor the people around me so that quality is shared by the whole team, not handed to QA at the end.

Shift-left by habit. In grooming and design sessions from day one, so quality is planned, not patched.
Automation that saves real time. Frameworks built to cut regression cycles, not to pad a test count.
A team player. Agile champion, mentor and the person who brings dev, product and ops to the same release table.

ai + qa

AI-powered quality engineering.

An area I am actively exploring, to make testing faster and smarter without lowering the bar.

AI will not replace good test design. It can take on the slow parts around it: drafting scenarios, reading noisy logs, and turning a red build into a clear bug report.

These are the ideas I am experimenting with. They are experiments, not production systems.
